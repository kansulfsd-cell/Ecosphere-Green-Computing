💻 Green Computing

Green Computing is the practice of using computers and technology in an environmentally friendly and energy-efficient way.

✅ Do's

  • Use energy-efficient devices
  • Turn off devices when not in use
  • Recycle old electronics
  • Use cloud storage wisely

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't waste electricity
  • Don't throw e-waste in dustbins
  • Avoid unnecessary printing
  • Don't keep devices charging all day
